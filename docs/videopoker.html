Rules & paytable (Jacks or Better, 9/6 demo)

Jacks or Better is a classic five-card draw video poker game. You are dealt five cards, choose which to hold, draw replacements once, and are paid if your final hand is a pair of Jacks or higher. This demo uses a standard 9/6 paytable (Full House pays 9× bet, Flush pays 6× bet).

HandExamplePayout (× bet)

Full HouseThree of a kind + a pair9
FlushFive cards, same suit6
StraightFive sequential ranks (A can be low)4

Jacks or BetterPair of J, Q, K, or A1

How a hand plays

  1. Deal: 5 cards from a shuffled 52-card deck (one deck per hand in this demo).
  2. Hold: keep any cards; unheld cards are replaced.
  3. Draw: receive replacements, hand is evaluated, payout applied.

Strategy note

Optimal play depends on the exact paytable and your dealt hand. A common baseline: hold made paying hands (JJ+ pair or better) unless drawing to a strong hand like four to a royal/straight flush. This demo is for entertainment and learning, not advantage play.

Frequently asked questions

Is this real-money gambling?
No. Neon Ace Video Poker is a free entertainment demo with virtual credits only — no deposits, withdrawals, prizes or real-money wagering.
What does “9/6” mean?
It refers to the paytable: a Full House pays 9× your bet and a Flush pays 6× your bet (per 1 unit). Paytables vary by machine; this demo shows its table clearly.
Can I hold all five cards?
Yes. If you hold all cards, the draw step keeps your hand as-is and evaluates it immediately.
How random is the shuffle?
Each hand uses a locally shuffled 52-card deck via the Web Crypto API for this static demo. It is not regulated or audited. Real-money products must use server-authoritative RNG and signed settlement.
Is it mobile-friendly and accessible?
Yes. Responsive layout, 44px hold/deal controls, canvas tap to hold, `aria-live` status, visible focus, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Rules and paytable are indexable HTML.
