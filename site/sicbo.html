Dice & totals

  • Three six-sided dice, totals range from 3 (1-1-1) to 18 (6-6-6).
  • A triple means all three dice show the same number.
  • Common table bets not shown in this lightweight demo: specific totals, doubles, combinations and single-die bets (payouts vary by house).

House edge context (typical)

Big/Small are usually among the lower-edge Sic Bo bets (~2.78% on standard tables), while specific triples are very high edge/high variance. Exact edges depend on the house paytable; this demo focuses on clear, educational play with virtual chips.

Why do Big/Small lose on a triple here?
Many Sic Bo tables treat any triple as a loss for Big/Small and Odd/Even (while offering separate triple bets). This demo follows that common rule and states it clearly on the table and in the rules.
